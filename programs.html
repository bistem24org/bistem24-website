Digital Content Development for Virtual Lab Activities
Higher Ed Virtual Labs
When integrated into instruction, virtual laboratory technologies can support student engagement in active hands-on engineering practice and design enabling conceptual and procedural knowledge development, with creativity and critical thinking skills.
​For example in designing this engineering course- Manufacturing Technology, interactive digital technology is employed to facilitate asynchronous and blended learning, with appropriate selection of meaningful project activity and virtual workshop. This ensures authentic learning and creates opportunities for real industry practice and development of problem-solving skills, which is essential to bolster engineering students’ readiness for the highly industrialized, digital global economy.
 The Wolfram simulation Software was used to perform the virtual lab activity in this design of introductory manufacturing technology course- for beginner/intermediate learners, other applications like the Deform 3D simulation software can be used at more advanced levels. 
Note: this training is on request for Higher Education institutions and organisations.
