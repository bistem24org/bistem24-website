01

STEM Outreach
& Engagement

As a member of the CDT's public engagement team, Olubunmi is involved in the development and delivery of outreach activities at science-based events for primary and secondary school pupils.

As a STEM Ambassador with STEM Learning since 2017, she has been involved in the delivery of STEM activities in school settings.

Prior to this, she worked in project support and operational roles in the healthcare, financial services, education and private sectors for sixteen years.

02

Education, Research
& Community

Olubunmi holds a BEng degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Benin, Nigeria, and an MEng degree in Mechanical Engineering with distinction from Glasgow Caledonian University, Scotland.

Her research interests involve the medical applications of ultrasound. Alongside her research, she volunteers as a committee member in charity organisations and community interest companies.

These organisations advocate for investment in girls' and women's health and educational development, across a diverse but connected spectrum of health and non-health issues.
