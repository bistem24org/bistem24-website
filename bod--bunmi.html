Olubunmi ONANUGA
​Postgraduate Researcher in the centre for doctoral training (CDT) in Ultrasonic Engineering at the University of Glasgow, Scotland.



As a member of the CDT’s public engagement team, Olubunmi is involved in the development and delivery of outreach activities at science-based events for primary and secondary school pupils. As a STEM Ambassador with STEM learning since 2017, she has been involved in the delivery of STEM activities in school settings. Prior to this, she had worked in project support and operational roles in the healthcare, financial services, education, and private sectors for sixteen years,
Olubunmi holds a BEng degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Benin, Nigeria and an MEng degree in Mechanical Engineering with distinction from Glasgow Caledonian University, Scotland. Alongside her research interest which involves the medical applications of ultrasound, she volunteers as a committee member in charity organisations and community interest companies. These organisations advocate for investment in girls’ and women’s health and educational development, across a diverse but connected spectrum of health and non-health issues.
