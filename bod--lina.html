Lina Eze
Group Managing Director at Tenece Technology Group

Lina has had a long career since 1999, working across various industries, first, as an information technology personnel in one of the big financial institutions in Nigeria. Second, as a business consultant and strategist involved in organizational restructuring across the manufacturing, oil & gas, and information technology industries. Third, as a business manager involved in the incubation start-up of various businesses across the Information technology, real estate, energy, and manufacturing industries.

She is currently a group managing director in a vertically integrated technology group with conglomerate logic in its periphery and a member of the board of directors of several businesses in Nigeria. Lina is also interested in social impact outcomes, such as women and girlchild empowerment, environmental protection and conservation, and recycling. In the past, Lina had worked with various educational institutions to bring awareness of ecological degradation and the need for protecting it by, for example, planting, thus encouraging students to plant a tree on every birthday to help the planet.

Lina holds a first-class degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Benin, Nigeria, a master’s in Environmental Diagnostics with distinction from Cranfield University, UK, another Master of Research in Management, and a PhD in Strategy and General Management, both from Esade Business School, Barcelona, Spain. She serves as the ChairPerson of the Board at Bi-STEM.
