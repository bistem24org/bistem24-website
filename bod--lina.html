BISTEM24 · BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LINA
EZE

Group Managing Director
at Tenece Technology Group

Lina has had a long career since 1999, working across various industries, first, as an information technology personnel in one of the big financial institutions in Nigeria.

Second, she worked as a business consultant and strategist involved in organizational restructuring across the manufacturing, oil and gas, and information technology industries.

Third, she worked as a business manager involved in the incubation and start-up of various businesses across the information technology, real estate, energy, and manufacturing industries.

She is currently a group managing director in a vertically integrated technology group with conglomerate logic in its periphery and a member of the board of directors of several businesses in Nigeria.

Lina is also interested in social impact outcomes, such as women and girl-child empowerment, environmental protection and conservation, and recycling.

In the past, Lina worked with various educational institutions to raise awareness of ecological degradation and the need to protect the environment. For example, she encouraged students to plant a tree on every birthday to help the planet.

Lina holds a first-class degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Benin, Nigeria, and a master’s in Environmental Diagnostics with distinction from Cranfield University, UK.

She also holds a Master of Research in Management and a PhD in Strategy and General Management, both from Esade Business School, Barcelona, Spain.

She serves as the Chairperson of the Board at Bi-STEM.
