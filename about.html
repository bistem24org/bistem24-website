The Vision
​Founded in 2020, BiSTEM24 EdTech Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to breaking the barriers to quality STEM education through a coherent vision to inspire and support the active and innovative use of technology to engage and create learning toward building subject-competent and digitally confident future graduates. Thus, improving learning outcomes through research, coaching, and creative learning design.
How We Do This…
by equipping teachers and educational stakeholders with instructional tools, digital skills and pedagogy, resources and strategies to create and produce quality teaching and authentic learning based on well researched instructional principles and strategies.
 Why?...
We believe that training one teacher, will impact the future of a thousand learners. Thus leveraging and providing access to digital for teachers, we create learning transformations, and competent 21st century graduates for positive and sustainable economic impact.
