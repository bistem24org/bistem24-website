01

Professional
Background

Temitayo Ojeleke was admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 1999. He obtained a Master's Degree in Oil and Gas Law from Robert Gordon University in Aberdeen, Scotland. An outstanding litigator, Tayo has argued before all superior courts of record in Nigeria and is considered to be an exceptional transactional lawyer with a particular bias for energy, maritime and property law.

He is the Hawkes Legal Principal Partner. Temitayo has led numerous teams in consulting on the acquisition of companies and concessions of various assets in Nigeria. He also serves as a legal adviser to several public and private sector organizations. He is a highly sought-after counsel of choice for several multinational organizations due to his dynamism and versatility.

Temitayo is also an expert on public-private partnerships, having consulted for both government and private entities on concession arrangements.

02

Professional
Memberships & Service

Temitayo is a member of the Nigerian Bar Association, the International Bar Association, and the Association of Independent Petroleum Negotiators.

He sits on the Governing Boards of several charitable organizations and private entities. He sits on the board of Directors at Bi-STEM as the Legal Adviser of the Organization, also availing her the privilege of having Hawkes Legal as the organization's Company Secretary.
