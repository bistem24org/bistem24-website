BISTEM24 EDTECH FOUNDATION

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

BiSTEM24 EdTech Foundation ("BiSTEM24", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, subject, and the message you submit. We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type and website usage data, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and messages.
  • Provide information about our educational initiatives and programmes.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Contact Forms and Email

If you submit a contact form, the information you provide may be sent to our designated email address so that we can respond to your enquiry. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through general contact forms.

4. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand usage, and improve the user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. If we introduce analytics or other tracking tools, this policy should be updated to describe their use accurately.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate our website, process enquiries, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

No method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may contact us to make a privacy-related request.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website without appropriate authorisation where required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
