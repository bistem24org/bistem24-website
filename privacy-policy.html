BISTEM24 EDTECH FOUNDATION

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we handle the information you share when using our website.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

BiSTEM24 EdTech Foundation ("BiSTEM24", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, subject, and message.

We may also receive limited technical information when you visit our website, depending on our hosting provider and the technologies used to operate the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your questions and enquiries.
  • Providing information about our programmes and initiatives.
  • Communicating with you when necessary.
  • Maintaining and improving our website.
  • Protecting our website and meeting applicable legal requirements.

3. Contact Forms and Email

When you submit a contact form on our website, the information you provide, such as your name, email address, subject, and message, is sent to our designated email inbox so we can review and respond to your enquiry.

Your submitted information may be processed by the email service used to receive and manage these messages, including Gmail where applicable.

Please avoid including passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information in general contact forms.

4. How We Handle Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We use the information you share to respond to enquiries and operate our website. We may share information with service providers when necessary to support these activities, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or meet legal obligations.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. These websites have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for how third-party websites collect or use information, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may contact us to ask about your information or make a privacy-related request.

8. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website without appropriate authorisation where required by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, practices, or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date where appropriate.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
